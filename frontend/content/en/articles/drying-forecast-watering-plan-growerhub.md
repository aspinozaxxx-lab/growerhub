Automation should explain the basis of a decision. GrowerHub now displays **an explained watering plan**. **By drying forecast** estimates how readings approach your dry anchor and a provisional time within permitted hours.

## What the plan considers

Inputs include confirmed [local anchors](/articles/poliv-po-mestnym-orientiram-growerhub/), fresh trends, response to normal watering and the last watering time. Permitted hours, minimum interval, daily limit and equipment availability constrain the result.

Expand **Plan · observation** below the scenario for duration, last watering, delivery time used today, soil data and reasons. Insufficient history produces **Waiting for data**.

The screenshot shows a training demo farm with reasons behind its provisional plan and a response established from history. If data is insufficient, this area explains why it is waiting.

## Try it

1. Assign a sensor and an admitted watering executor to the greenhouse.
2. Choose **By drying forecast**, enter and confirm your own anchors.
3. Set permitted hours, checked duration, interval and limit. Save **Observe without commands**.
4. Enable observation and compare the plan with soil condition and usual care.

Observation does not deliver water. Control is explicit and retains equipment and data checks.

## Provisional times can change

The plan is revised with fresh data and checked before execution. Stale readings, an unreliable trend, missing response confirmation or reached limits cause waiting with a reason. No response does not start endless top-ups.

**The dose remains fixed by the user. Weather adjustment is not applied at this stage.** A sensor line cannot replace evaluating the water your plants need.

Without a sensor, use [scheduled watering](/articles/poliv-po-raspisaniyu-growerhub/). Explore the shared demo farm without equipment.
