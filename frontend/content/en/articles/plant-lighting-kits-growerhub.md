A small nursery has plenty of recurring jobs: switching lights, ventilating, checking soil and getting water to the pots. GrowerHub brings control and observations into one place. Start with one smart plug, then add a soil sensor and watering to suit your plants.

Explore the project on the [home page](/en/), then choose a kit or request help in the [catalog](/en/equipment/). If you do not need hardware yet, choose **“Try without hardware”**: the demo farm includes virtual devices, automations and sensor history.

## What smart plugs help with

A suitable plug can switch a lamp, extractor fan, compatible pump or another appliance. When choosing equipment with you, we will ask what you want to connect, check the electrical load and select a suitable way to control it.

- **Lighting:** set operating hours for seedlings, cuttings or mature plants, then adjust them from your phone.
- **Ventilation:** manage an extractor fan alongside the rest of your equipment. We will choose an automation that suits your setup and sensors.
- **Watering:** discuss the pump, water delivery and run limits. We will check the layout and device operation before setting up a suitable automation.
- **Electricity:** if the plug provides measurements, GrowerHub can show power and energy history. Compare days and notice when an appliance runs longer or uses more electricity.

## A soil sensor: see how your potting mix dries

A sensor helps compare readings with plant condition and watering history. For example, see how quickly small pots dry compared with larger ones, or how the readings change after watering. Following changes in your own potting mix is more useful than expecting one ideal number to suit every plant.

![Illustration of a soil-moisture sensor beside plants](/content/nursery/soil-sensor.webp)

**The soil sensor costs 499 RUB.** Add it to your basket [from the catalog](/en/equipment/#soil-sensor), separately or with a kit. You can mention where it will be used: in a pot, a bed or a greenhouse. We will discuss the connection and first setup by phone; there is no online payment.

## A pump on its own or with drippers

Order our pump **on its own for 1,999 RUB** or **with 20 drippers and tubing for 2,499 RUB**. The pump without drippers can be selected for your own layout; the kit helps run water lines to your pots.

![Watering-kit illustration: pump, drippers and tubing](/content/nursery/pump-drip-kit.webp)

The pump supports **Wi-Fi without a Zigbee coordinator or Zigbee**. Zigbee requires a compatible coordinator, which is not included with either the pump or the watering kit. We will discuss the connection mode, contents and first setup steps by phone.

Add the [pump](/en/equipment/#pump) or [watering kit](/en/equipment/#pump-drip-kit) to your basket, separately or alongside other devices. Describe your pot count, distances and water source in the comment. Assembly starts after order confirmation; we will agree on delivery during the call.

Before running it, we will choose delivery settings and run limits, check distribution between drippers and arrange a way to stop the water. A set of 20 drippers does not mean any 20 plants need the same watering.

## Place an order request or ask for help

Kits and individual devices follow the same steps:

1. Choose a kit, sensor or pump and add it to your basket. Combine different items in one order and adjust quantities before submitting.
2. Enter your name and phone number. Telegram and a comment are optional. The comment is a useful place to describe the appliances you want to connect.
3. Find a convenient pickup point on the [CDEK website](https://www.cdek.ru/ru/offices/). Enter its city, **point code and address**. GrowerHub does not select the pickup point automatically.
4. Read the request terms and submit the order.
5. Keep the request number shown on screen. We will call to discuss the contents, compatibility, first setup and delivery. Assembly starts after order confirmation. **There is nothing to pay now.**

If you are unsure what to choose, select **“Help me choose”**. Your name and phone number let us respond; a description of your plants and appliances helps us choose the contents. Asking for advice does not require a purchase.

No account is required. If the connection fails during submission, retry in the same form: it checks the original request instead of creating a duplicate. Keep the tab open until the outcome is confirmed.

## What you need at home

For PushOk kits, you need a phone and home Wi-Fi with internet access. Connect the hub to your network using Upravlyator, then link it to GrowerHub using its ID and pairing button. This route does not require a separate computer or Home Assistant. Follow the [getting-started guide](/en/getting-started/).

We will check the first appliance together. You can then set up a suitable automation and explore the available history. Add other appliances gradually; you do not need to move the entire nursery at once.

## What we will check before connecting

- The appliance model, load and surroundings: lamps, fans and pumps must be chosen for their specifications. Control and measurement capabilities depend on the particular device.
- For watering, the water-delivery layout, run limits and a way to stop it. A smart plug alone does not replace preparing the watering system.
- Hub and device placement. White has a signal amplifier, but range depends on walls and positioning.
- GrowerHub and the PushOk cloud need internet access. Without a connection, new GrowerHub commands will not reach the device.

Start with the job that most often takes you away from your plants. We will help you choose the equipment and a useful first automation.
