With several growing shelves, adjusting each timer separately becomes inconvenient. GrowerHub brings lighting schedules together: give seedlings, cuttings and mature plants different times, then adjust them from your phone.

We have redesigned the [home page](/en/) and put together [three kits](/en/equipment/) so your first step does not require learning technical abbreviations. If you do not need equipment yet, choose **“Try without equipment”** to explore the demo farm with virtual devices and history.

## Choose your first kit

| Kit | Contents | Kit price |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Your first shelf | PushOk Mini POK101, one smart plug | 3,290 RUB |
| More connection headroom | PushOk White POK100, one smart plug | 4,490 RUB |
| Three lighting zones | PushOk White POK100, three smart plugs | 5,490 RUB |

Each kit includes a power supply and cable, device checks, clear instructions and help with the first connection. **Use your own lamps. Shipping is separate**; we agree the cost and timing before sending the kit. Check the catalog and basket for the current price before submitting a request.

![Illustration of the first-shelf kit: flat Mini hub, one smart plug, power supply and cable](/content/nursery/kit-mini.webp)

Kit images illustrate the contents. The exact plug, power supply and cable may look different. Hub photographs and manufacturer links are in the [equipment catalog](/en/equipment/zigbee-coordinators/).

If you are unsure, choose **“Help me choose”**. Leave your name and phone number; optionally describe your shelves and lamps. You do not need to order a kit to ask for help.

## Place an order request

1. Choose a kit and add it to your basket. Adjust quantities before submitting.
2. Enter your name and phone number. Telegram and a comment are optional.
3. Find a convenient pickup point on the [CDEK website](https://www.cdek.ru/ru/offices/). Enter its city, **point code and address**. GrowerHub does not provide a map or automatic pickup-point selection.
4. Read the request terms and submit the order.
5. Keep the request number shown on screen. **There is nothing to pay now.** We will contact you to confirm the kit and delivery.

No account is required. If the connection fails during submission, retry in the same form: it checks the original request instead of creating a duplicate. Keep the tab open until the outcome is confirmed.

## What you need at home

You need a phone, home Wi-Fi with internet access and your lamps. Set up the hub with Upravlyator, then connect it to GrowerHub using its ID and the pairing button. This route does not require a separate computer or Home Assistant. Follow the [getting-started guide](/en/getting-started/).

Start by checking one lamp, then set its schedule with our help. You can add other zones gradually rather than moving the entire nursery at once.

## Tested equipment and limits

- A **POK100v2 running firmware 95 with a Zbeacon TS011F plug** has been tested in GrowerHub for readings, states, on/off control and reconnection.
- **Mini POK101 has not yet been physically verified in GrowerHub.** We check it and the specific plug revision before confirming supply. Kits are assembled after confirmation; we do not promise immediate shipment from stock.
- White has a signal amplifier; actual range depends on walls, placement and devices. We can discuss placement before you order.
- GrowerHub schedules require internet access and the PushOk cloud. They do not replace a physical cutoff or guarantee offline operation.
- These are **lighting kits**. Connecting a pump to a smart plug alone does not make watering safe. Lamp loads and operating conditions must be checked separately.

Start with one useful task, such as turning the first shelf's lights on at the right time. Add more when you need it.
